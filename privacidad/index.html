03Why we are allowed to

We process your data because it is necessary to provide the service you requested or to take steps before contracting; because you gave us your consent (for example when you connect a social account or accept notifications, which you can withdraw at any time); because we have a legitimate interest in running and securing our business; or because the law requires us to keep certain records.

06Cookies and local storage

The public website uses no advertising or analytics cookies. It stores your language preference in your browser's local storage so the site opens in your language next time. The client platform uses the cookies strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to protect your session. Videos embedded from Instagram and YouTube on our website are loaded from those platforms, which may set their own cookies under their own policies.

07How long we keep it

  • Project files (raw material, working files, deliveries): 14 days after each delivery or revision, as described in our Terms and Conditions. Download your final videos when you approve them.
  • Account and project records (orders, proposals, comments, invoices): while your account is active and afterwards for as long as required for accounting, tax and legal purposes.
  • Enquiries that do not become a project: for as long as they may be relevant to a future collaboration. You can ask us to delete them at any time.
  • Job applications: up to 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them earlier.
  • Backups: encrypted daily backups are kept for 35 days; monthly backups for up to 400 days. Deleted data may persist in backups until they expire.
  • Technical logs: for a short period, as needed for security and troubleshooting.

08Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored with access controls that separate what each role can see (clients see only their own projects; editors do not see clients' contact or financial data). We offer two-step verification for accounts, keep encrypted backups, review our security regularly and limit access to our team on a need-to-know basis. Payment proofs are stored in a private bucket and served only through short-lived links. No system is completely secure, so if we ever become aware of a breach affecting your data we will inform you without undue delay.

09Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy.
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your data, when we no longer need it for the service or for a legal obligation.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Withdraw a consent you previously gave (for example, disconnect a social account or stop notifications).
  • Receive your data in a portable format.

Write to agency@voxenvisuals.com and we will answer within 30 days.

10International transfers

Our providers store and process data in different countries, mainly South America (database) and the United States (hosting, video, email and AI services). Where a transfer takes place, we rely on the safeguards offered by each provider (contractual clauses and security certifications) so that your data stays protected wherever it is processed.

11Minors

Our services and platform are intended for people aged 18 or over, or for minors acting through a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or providers change. The current version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will notify active clients through the platform or by email.

Los comprobantes de pago se guardan en un bucket privado y se sirven solo mediante enlaces de corta duración. Ningún sistema es completamente seguro, así que si alguna vez detectamos una brecha que afecte tus datos te informaremos sin demoras indebidas.
